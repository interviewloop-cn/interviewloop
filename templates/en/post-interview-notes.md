# Post-interview Notes · <date> · <role category>

> Fill in within 30 minutes of the interview ending. Record only what happened; no feelings, no evaluations.
> Record interviewer reactions as behaviour only: what they asked, what they followed up on, whether they interrupted, what they did at the close. Do not record attitude, tone or facial expression (PROTOCOL B-2).
> Incomplete notes still run: the model asks only for the 3–5 missing items that matter (PROTOCOL B-1). With a recording or transcript, the skill drafts this template from the transcript, each count sourced to a verbatim line, for you to confirm.
> The three structured count fields at the bottom are the only basis for Module B's hard signals. Left blank = the corresponding signal is "cannot judge"; do not estimate, do not count from the narrative.
> A transcript is optional input. If one exists, attach it as a separate file; do not paste transcript text into this template.
