# Business stage × what is being assessed (cn)

> This is a translation of the cn market package with cultural notes; it is not a separate market package. Where it differs from the Chinese file, the Chinese file is authoritative.
> Module A-4 uses this table to judge the business stage (the only admissible basis is [JD]), then combines it with `interviewer-types.md` to make the type forecast.
> The "your ammunition" column is empty; the user fills it in the personal handbook or a local copy. The repository version is always empty.

| Business stage | What is being assessed | Evidence shape they want | Common types | Your ammunition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Greenfield** (0→1) | Framework building, trial-and-error with stop-loss, operating independently | Plan comparison + hypothesis list + validation path + switch conditions | A | |
| **Optimisation** (mature) | Rigour in stated figures and wording, attribution, cost structure, ceiling | Absolute values + conversion basis + attribution breakdown | B | |
| **Platform** (strategy / platform) | Execution layer or mechanism layer, transferability | Mechanism-level evidence | C | |
| **Advisory** (strategy / staff role) | Mapping out models, evaluation and falsification | Framework × metrics × falsification | F | |
| **Rebuild under new constraints** (new industry / new regulation) | Whether you can rebuild a plan under a new industry's new constraints, and how large the industry-language gap is | Ask about the current state and no-go areas first → framework split by route → moves inside the red lines | A-2 | |
| **Talent allocation** (when the role is mismatched) | Where your real capability band is, and where to place you | A truthful boundary of authority + a clear statement of direction | G | |
| **HR round** | Stability, motive, levelling, start date | Portable one-liner + consistent stated figures and wording | D | |

JD clues for judging the stage:
- Greenfield: the JD contains "build", "from 0", "new business", "new channel", with no existing baseline metrics
- Optimisation: the JD gives explicit baseline metrics and improvement targets (ROI, cost, conversion rate)
- Platform: the JD emphasises "mechanism", "system", "reusable", "cross-team"
- Advisory: the JD emphasises "research", "insight", "evaluation", "reporting", with no direct accountability for business results
- Rebuild under new constraints: the JD explicitly requires a cross-industry background or flags heavy regulation
- Talent allocation: cannot be seen from the JD; only exposed in the interview by Type G

## Cultural notes

This taxonomy was written from experienced-hire interviews in the Chinese internet industry. The following items may not hold in other markets.

- In the HR round, on salary: the protocol forbids guessing a market range because Chinese job posts often omit salary. In jurisdictions where salary ranges are legally disclosed in postings, the "cannot judge" default for the salary row applies less often.
- "Departure narrative" (离职口径) in this taxonomy is about a consistent story for why you left. In markets with contractual notice periods, the question "when can you start" is a contractual fact rather than a negotiation signal, so "start date" in the HR round row may not carry the same meaning.
