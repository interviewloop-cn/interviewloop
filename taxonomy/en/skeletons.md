# Response skeletons A–J (cn)

> This is a translation of the cn market package with cultural notes; it is not a separate market package. Where it differs from the Chinese file, the Chinese file is authoritative.
> A skeleton is the structure of an answer, not the answer. The protocol does not generate full scripts; "say it this way next time" only points to a skeleton code plus one line of structure.
> All example sentences are fictional (fictional candidate: a clinical-marketing role in orthopaedic implants); they only demonstrate structure, and the numbers must not be cited. Jargon and orders of magnitude are calibrated against `example/SOURCES.md`.

---

## Skeleton A · Judgement + basis + reversal condition

Used for: Types A / C; any "how do you judge", "why did you do it this way".

```
My judgement is X. The basis is [data / experiment / competitor research]. If [metric] falls below [threshold], the judgement was wrong and I switch to [fallback plan].
```

Example (fictional): "My judgement is to put the second-half budget first into the 18 hospitals among the 62 VBP-awarded hospitals that have not yet done a first case. The basis is that in the 12 months since the award their purchases were zero while their orthopaedic outpatient volume is in the region's top 40%. If fewer than 10 of them have gone live after two quarters, the judgement was wrong and the budget goes back to ramping the hospitals already live."

## Skeleton B · Attribution breakdown

Used for: Type B; any "which part of this growth is attributed to what".

1. **Time-sequenced split**: roll the moves out in phases, and book each phase's increment separately
2. **Funnel location**: lead → first touch → effective conversation → close; convert each stage to the final metric
3. **Honest fallback**: "the overlapping part cannot be fully attributed; the combined moves are judged on overall ROI, and single stages are used only for stop-loss decisions"

Example (fictional): "Monthly knee case volume from 310 to 540. The 120 cases added in the first quarter after the VBP award are booked as the price effect; the proctoring centre went live in the second quarter, booked as 70 cases; the remaining 40 overlap, and I don't force the attribution. I look at the return on the combined actions as a whole and use single steps only to decide whether to stop."

## Skeleton C · Controlled concession

Used for: all types; reason for leaving, biggest weakness, gap items.

```
[Specific area] is an industry-language gap; I will close it within [time limit]; the underlying [method] is transferable.
```

Example (fictional): "Pricing of the robot's single-use accessories and getting the charge to land under DRG grouping is an industry-language gap; I'll close it within two months; the underlying procedure-adoption pathway is transferable."

**Never concede**: flaws of the delivery, structure or data kind; never talk the role down ("responsible for supporting X" and "assisting with Y" both talk the role down).

## Skeleton D · Portable one-liner

Used for: Type D; any evidence the other party needs to relay to a third person.

```
Action + one number + a status word.
```

Example (fictional): "Unicompartmental knee arthroplasty surgeon training pathway: 48 surgeons completed a first case, procedure penetration from 9% to 17%."

## Skeleton E · Boundary close

Used for: Type C; anywhere your capability boundary is asked about.

```
On this I go as far as the [application layer / strategy layer]; X at the [engineering layer / specialist layer] I can't judge, and I need [role] to work with me.
```

Example (fictional): "On this I go as far as the clinical-marketing layer; X in the robot's navigation accuracy and the registration certificate's indications I can't judge, and I need clinical support and regulatory affairs to work with me."

## Skeleton F · Framework + evaluation + falsification

Used for: Type F; any "what do you think of X", "how would you evaluate / how would you falsify".

```
Enumerate and restate → framework → a metric for each dimension → falsification condition → a case fills one slot (≤ 90 seconds)
```

Example (fictional): "You're asking about three layers: listing, first case, ramp-up. For listing I look at coverage of VBP-awarded hospitals, and the falsification condition is no growth over two quarters; for first case I look at first-case conversion; for ramp-up I look at monthly case volume; for the case I'll only cover the first-case layer."

## Skeleton G · Org-structure reverse question

Used for: all types, **ask one in every interview**. The purpose is to verify the difficulty of collaboration and the real power of the role, not courtesy. Pick one of three:

- "Is the unit this role sits in parallel to [the adjacent unit], or under the same owner?"
- "Does the role have a mechanism or metric of its own that it is accountable for?"
- "Does the direct manager still run business / projects themselves, or only allocate resources?"

## Skeleton H · Pre-case retrieval

Used for: Type A-2; any "if it were you, how would you do it". Three questions first, **the order must not be reversed**:

1. "What is the current state / composition of this area for you right now?" (know what they are doing first)
2. "Is there anything that explicitly cannot be done?" (know the no-go areas first)
3. "What is the profile of the target user?" (only then ask about the people)

Then: framework split by route, each route ≤ 90 seconds, write the red lines into the plan, close within 3 minutes.
If you ask about the profile before the current state, the plan you produce will land on channels they do not use at all.

## Skeleton I · Direction question

Used for: Type G; any "where do you want to go".

```
My capability band is [X]. The challenge I want is not to change fields, but to push the ceiling of this band and then replicate it for others — [the role / team they just mentioned] is exactly that.
```

Must not appear: "comfort zone", "I'd like to try something I haven't done", "it's all the same to me".

## Skeleton J · Implicit user question

Used for: any "do you use / buy / play it yourself".

```
I'm not a core user. When I was in [previous industry] I wasn't [that industry's target user] either — I break down users through the decision chain, not by being a user myself. [If applicable] Personally I [brief fact], but that is not the basis on which I understand users.
```

Example (fictional): "I'm not a core user. I was an orthopaedic resident for a year and have scrubbed in, but for the five years since I haven't been the surgeon — I understand users by breaking down the surgeon's decision chain, not by being one."

## Cultural notes

This taxonomy was written from experienced-hire interviews in the Chinese internet industry. The following items may not hold in other markets.

- Skeleton D (portable one-liner) and Skeleton C (controlled concession) assume that the HRBP relays your evidence to a business owner. This relay structure differs where the recruiter is not part of the hiring decision, so the assumption behind these two skeletons may not hold there.
- The 90-second / 3-minute case limits in Skeletons F and H were set against Chinese interview slots of 35–45 minutes; where slots are longer or shorter, the limits may not hold.
